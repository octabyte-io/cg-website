## Getting started

### What is Cloudgate?

A deployment platform you point at your own Servers and your own AWS or Google Cloud account. It
builds and runs Applications, databases, Compose stacks and AI models there, with domains,
certificates, backups, monitoring and access control. Start with the [Quickstart](/docs/quickstart).

### Do I need my own server?

You need a Server or a Cloud account before anything deploys. Any Ubuntu machine you can reach over
SSH as root, or as a user with passwordless sudo, works. On the Hosted instance you can also buy one
from Cloudgate in the console. See [Servers](/docs/guide/servers) and [Bought Servers](/docs/guide/bought-servers).

### Do I need a domain name?

No. A free sslip.io address that points at your Server works straight away, over plain HTTP. Add your
own domain when you want HTTPS. See [Domains](/docs/guide/domains).

### Can I deploy without a Dockerfile?

Yes. Nixpacks, the default builder, works out how to build your code from its files. Railpack, Heroku and
Paketo buildpacks, static files, a Dockerfile or a ready-made Docker image are the other options.
See [Applications](/docs/guide/applications).

## Plans and pricing

### How much does the Hosted instance cost?

Starter is 15 USD a month and Business 35 USD a month. Each includes 3 Servers and 3 Cloud
Environments; an Add-on adds one more of each for 5 USD. Members, Projects and Environments on
Servers are not counted. See [pricing](/pricing).

### Is there a free trial?

Yes, 30 days on either plan. A card is needed to start it, and there is one trial per Organization,
person and card. Buying a Server is charged at once, even during the trial. See [Billing](/docs/guide/billing).

### What happens if I go over my plan's count?

Only the next addition is refused. Nothing that already runs is switched off. Buy an Add-on, or
upgrade, to add more.

### What does Business add over Starter?

Single sign-on, SCIM, custom roles, forward auth in front of your apps, and paying for other
Organizations on the same plan. The counts are the same.

## Self-hosting

### Is Cloudgate open source?

No. It is source-available under the Elastic License 2.0. You may use, self-host and change it, but
you may not offer it to others as a hosted service, or get around its license checks. See
[Self-hosting Cloudgate](/docs/self-host).

### Is self-hosting free?

Yes. The free version deploys everything Cloudgate can deploy, with one Organization and one build at
a time per Server. An Enterprise license unlocks the rest. You pay only for the servers you run.

### How do I get the source?

Clone it from the public repository,
[github.com/octabyte-io/cloudgate](https://github.com/octabyte-io/cloudgate). No account or access
request is needed. [Install Cloudgate](/docs/self-host/install) takes you from the clone to a
signed-in console.

## Deploying

### Can I deploy from my terminal or CI?

Yes. The CLI, `cloudgate deploy`, uploads a folder to an Application and follows its Deployment. In
CI, set `CLOUDGATE_KEY` to a Personal key. See the [CLI guide](/docs/cli) and [CI use](/docs/cli/ci).

### Can I deploy a monorepo with several apps in one command?

Yes. Link its root with `cloudgate link --whole-project`, which makes it a Project folder holding the
Project config as `cloudgate.yaml`. Give each app `source: { folder: apps/web }`, then one
`cloudgate deploy` there applies the config's Plan and builds every app from its subfolder. See
[deploy a whole Project](/docs/cli#deploy-a-whole-project-from-one-folder).

### Can I roll back a bad deploy?

Yes, for an Application with **Keep rollback points** turned on: open its **Rollbacks** tab and press
**Roll back** on an earlier point. Databases and Compose stacks have no rollback. See
[Deployments and rollbacks](/docs/guide/deployments).

### Can I move a Service to another Server?

No. A Service's Server or Cloud account is fixed when it is made. To run it somewhere else, create a
new Service in the new place.

### Do Services inherit Project variables?

No. A Service gets only its own variables and reads a Project or Environment value by naming it,
such as `${{project.KEY}}`. See [Environment variables](/docs/guide/environment-variables).

## Security and access

### Are my secrets safe?

Secrets are encrypted at rest, API answers say only whether a secret is set, and every Agent result
passes a mask that replaces stored secrets with dots. See [Security](/security).

### How do I report a bug or a security problem?

Open an issue on the [cloudgate repository](https://github.com/octabyte-io/cloudgate/issues) for a
bug or a feature idea. Never report a security problem in a public issue: on the repository's
**Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability**, which only the maintainers can see. Questions
about your account go to [support@octabyte.io](mailto:support@octabyte.io).

### Can I limit a member to one Project?

Yes. A member reaches only the Projects, Environments and Services an owner or admin grants under
**Manage access**. Owners and admins reach everything. See [Members and roles](/docs/guide/members-and-roles).

### Is there single sign-on?

Yes, with OIDC providers, on the Business plan or with an Enterprise license on a self-hosted
instance. SCIM provisioning comes with it. See [SSO and SCIM](/docs/guide/sso-and-scim).

## AI agents

### Can an AI agent deploy for me?

Yes. Create an Agent with the Operator role and connect your AI tool to the MCP endpoint with its
key. It can deploy, start, stop, cancel, and ask for previews and Plans within its grant. See
[Agents](/docs/guide/agents) and [Connect an AI tool](/docs/mcp/connect).

### What can an Agent never do?

Apply a Plan, change variables, settings or access, configure health checks, or create an AI model.
It never becomes an owner or admin, and every call it makes is in the audit log. See
[MCP guard rails](/docs/mcp/guard-rails).

### Can I run my own AI models?

Yes. An AI model is an Application that serves one model with Ollama, vLLM or llama.cpp, optionally
on whole GPUs, reached through Cloudgate's OpenAI-compatible Gateway. See
[AI models](/docs/guide/ai-models) and the [AI agents overview](/ai-agents).
