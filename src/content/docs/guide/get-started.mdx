The wizard lives at `/get-started` on the console. You normally reach it from a **Deploy** button on
the [template catalogue](https://cgt.octabyte.io/), which opens it with that app already chosen.
Everything it needs to remember is kept in the page address, so you can reload, go through
payment or confirm your email and land back where you were.

## What you can do

- Deploy any app from the template catalogue in three steps, without writing any configuration.
- Run it on a Server you already added, a server you buy from Cloudgate (Hosted only), or a server
  you rent elsewhere, such as a VPS.
- Get a working web address at once, with passwords and keys the app needs made for you.

## Deploy an app

<Steps>
<Step title="Pick an app and sign in">
On the template catalogue website, open the app and press its **Deploy** button. If you are not
signed in, the console asks you to. **Create an account** keeps the app you picked: on the Hosted
instance, following the link in the confirmation email signs you in and brings you back to the
wizard.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose where it should run">
The wizard asks **Where should Ghost run?** (with your app's name). It offers what fits best first:

- If your Organization already has a Server that is ready, **Use fra-1** (with your Server's
  name) puts the app there.
- **Get a server from Cloudgate**, marked **Recommended**, buys a server for you. This is on the
  Hosted instance only; see [Buy a Server from Cloudgate](/docs/guide/bought-servers).
- **Use my own server** is for a server you already rent. You need its address and SSH access
  to it.

<Screenshot name="get-started-where" alt="The get-started wizard asking where the app should run, with the options Get a server from Cloudgate and Use my own server" caption="A Server you already have is offered first; otherwise you pick one of the two ways to get one." />
</Step>
<Step title="Connect your own server (if you chose it)">
Fill in **Your server**: the address, SSH port, SSH user, and a name. If your Organization has no
SSH key yet, press **Make a key**. The wizard then shows one line to run on your server, which adds
Cloudgate's public key to `~/.ssh/authorized_keys`:

```bash
mkdir -p ~/.ssh && echo "ssh-ed25519 AAAA... cloudgate" >> ~/.ssh/authorized_keys
```

Copy the exact line from the wizard (it holds your real key), run it on the server, then press
**Save and continue** and **Connect my server**. Progress shows **Signed in to your server**,
then **Installing what apps need**, then **Ready**.
</Step>
<Step title="Or wait for your bought server">
If you chose to buy one, pay at checkout. The wizard then shows **Payment**, **Creating your
server**, **Setting it up** and **Ready to use**. Nothing deploys until the server is ready.
Reloading the page is safe: the wizard picks up where it was.
</Step>
<Step title="Fill in the details and deploy">
The last step asks only for the values the app needs. **Passwords and keys it needs are made for
you.** A summary, **What Deploy makes**, lists the Server, a **New Project** named after the app
and the **Web address** (**Made for you**). Press **Deploy Ghost** (with your app's name).
</Step>
<Step title="Open your app">
When the app is running, the wizard shows its address, a button to open it, **Open the Project**,
and **What next**. Open the address to finish the app's own first-run setup.
</Step>
</Steps>

What you get: a new Project named after the app, with a `production` Environment on your Server,
and the app's Services deployed in it. From here it is an ordinary Project: see
[Projects and Environments](/docs/guide/projects-and-environments).

<Callout type="note" title="When the app does not start by itself">
Cloudgate applies the setup for you, as you, once the Server is ready, but only if the
Environment's [Plan](/docs/guide/project-config) holds nothing but what the wizard asked for. If
the Plan holds more, it is left for you to review and **Apply**, and the wizard says why. A
refused Apply also says why.
</Callout>

## Add HTTPS with your own domain

The address the wizard makes is a free `sslip.io` host, served over plain HTTP. To serve the app
over HTTPS, add your own domain to its Service, for example `shop.northwind.example`, and point
its DNS at the Server. See [Domains and HTTPS](/docs/guide/domains).

## If you have no plan yet

On the Hosted instance, an Organization without a plan starts the Starter free trial on the way:

- **Use my own server** sends you to checkout first (**Start your 30-day free trial**). A card is
  needed.
- **Get a server from Cloudgate** starts the trial as part of the purchase. The server itself is
  charged at once; it has no trial. See [Billing and plans](/docs/guide/billing).

## What you cannot do

<Callout type="limit" title="Who can use the wizard">
The wizard adds a Server, so you need a role that may create Servers (owner or admin, or a custom
role that allows it). A member without it is told to ask the Organization's owner or admin. See
[Members and roles](/docs/guide/members-and-roles).
</Callout>

- Buying a server is offered only on the Hosted instance, and only where buying is set up.
- The wizard always makes a new Project. To add a template to an existing Project, use
  [Templates](/docs/guide/templates) in the console instead.
- Cloudgate cannot apply the setup by itself for a Project whose config is kept in a repository;
  you paste the template's part into your `cloudgate.yaml` instead.
- An app installed from a template gets no updates from the catalogue afterwards.
