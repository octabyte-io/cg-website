A Bought Server is a Server Octabyte runs for you. Once it is ready it works like any other
[Server](/docs/guide/servers): you deploy Applications, databases and Compose stacks onto it, open its
shell and read its telemetry. Cloudgate makes its key and runs its setup by itself.

## What can you do with a Bought Server?

- Buy one by location, size and image, from a world map, with a price before you pay.
- See every Bought Server on one page, with its stage, size and what it costs a month.
- Turn on daily backups of the Server's disk, take one more with **Back up now**, and restore the
  Server from any backup kept.
- Add extra storage from 10 GB to 10 TB, and grow it later.
- Change its size up or down among the sizes sold where it is.
- Delete it, with the unused part of the month credited.

<Callout type="limit" title="Hosted only, owners and admins">
**Buy a Server**, the **Bought Servers** page and the **Bought Server** tab show only on the Hosted
instance, at cloudgate.octabyte.app, where buying a Server is offered. On a self-hosted instance,
[add your own Server over SSH](/docs/guide/servers). Seeing them needs the role that may see Servers.
Buying, changing, backing up and restoring a Bought Server needs the owner or admin role, or a custom
role that allows adding Servers; the controls are greyed out for others and say why.
</Callout>

## How do I buy a Server?

Press **Buy a Server**, pick a location, a size and an image, add any extras, and pay. Cloudgate then
creates the machine and runs its setup for you:

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Buy a Server">
In **Servers** or **Bought Servers**, press **Buy a Server**. The
[get-started wizard](/docs/guide/get-started) offers the same choices in its first step.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick a location">
**Suggested** offers **Closest** and **Cheapest**: the least a Server with at least 4 GB of memory costs
near you, and anywhere. Or pick under **1. Location**: a pin on the map or a city card, nearest first,
each with its distance. Your position is first guessed from your browser's time zone; **Show where I
am** asks your browser for its location, and it stays on the page.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick a size">
Under **2. Size in** the chosen city, choose a family, then a size. Each row gives vCPUs, memory, disk
and the price per month. A size not sold there right now is greyed out.

| Family | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Standard** | Shared Intel or AMD cores, the cheapest, enough for most apps. |
| **Performance** | Shared AMD cores, faster than Standard, in more locations. |
| **Arm** | Shared Arm cores, the cheapest per core. Your images must run on Arm. |
| **Dedicated** | Cores reserved for this Server alone, for steady heavy load. |

<Screenshot name="servers-buy" alt="Buy a Server with Suggested cards for Closest, Ashburn, VA at $14 a month, and Cheapest, Nuremberg at $9 a month, a world map with pins for six cities, city cards with distances from near New York and Ashburn, VA selected, and the Performance sizes in Ashburn from 2 vCPU, 4 GB RAM, 80 GB disk at $14 to 16 vCPU, 32 GB RAM, 360 GB disk at $83 a month" caption="Your position is guessed from the browser's time zone; city cards are ordered nearest first." />
</Step>
<Step title="Pick the image">
**3. Server image** lists only the images the size's architecture can run, and starts on Ubuntu 24.04.
</Step>
<Step title="Add extras">
Under **4. Extras**:

| Extra | What it is | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Backups** | A copy of the Server's own disk every day; the last 7 are kept. | Priced once you pick a size. Extra storage is not included. |
| **Extra storage** | A separate disk, in GB, mounted at `/mnt/storage`. | 10 GB to 10 TB. Can grow later, never shrink. Leave empty for none. |
</Step>
<Step title="Check the Summary and buy">
The **Summary** lists the Server, backups and extra storage with their monthly prices, your plan, and
the **Total per month**. **Today** is what is charged now, for the part of the month up to your plan's
renewal. Tick that you accept Octabyte's terms and acceptable-use policy, then press **Buy**. If the
Organization has no plan yet, the button reads **Buy and start Free trial** and starts the trial with
the purchase. The line under the button says what is still missing.

<Screenshot name="servers-buy-summary" alt="The Summary card for a 4 vCPU, 8 GB Server in Ashburn, VA on Ubuntu 24.04 at $25 with Backups at $5, a total of $30 per month, Today $18.00 explaining that during the Free trial the Server is charged on its own invoice from November 27 to December 15, the terms ticked and the Buy button" caption="Today is the part of the month charged now; during a Free trial it runs to the trial's end." />
</Step>
<Step title="Wait until it is ready">
When a payment page is needed you are sent there and back; your bank may ask you to confirm. The
Server is then created and set up by itself. Its **Setup** card reads **Setting up**, and nothing can
deploy to it until setup finishes. The first setup waits up to ten minutes for the new machine to accept
SSH.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note" title="When your Server count is full">
If the Organization already has all the Servers its plan allows, the Summary adds an Add-on line ("room
for 1 more Server") and the Add-on is bought with the Server. See [Billing](/docs/guide/billing).
</Callout>

### Finish or cancel an unpaid purchase

A purchase left at the payment step shows in **Servers being bought** on the Servers screen, and on the
Bought Servers page, as **Waiting for payment**. **Finish paying** returns you to the payment; on the
Servers screen, **Cancel** drops it and nothing is charged. Rows also show **Creating** and, while one
is removed, **Deleting**. If creating the machine fails, what you paid is given back in full.

## Where do I see all my Bought Servers?

Open **Bought Servers** in the Organization rail, under **Servers**. The page lists every Bought Server
in the Organization with what it costs, under one sentence such as "2 Bought Servers, $47/month on
your plan's invoice." A Server still waiting for payment is counted apart ("and 1 waiting for
payment") and left out of the total; one being deleted is left out of the count and the total. The
row shows from the moment buying is offered, before anything is bought, so **Buy a Server** is always
one click away.

<Screenshot name="bought-servers-list" alt="The Bought Servers page with its summary sentence and a table of Bought Servers, each with its Location under its name, its stage, size, backups, monthly price and a Manage button" caption="One sentence for the count and the monthly total, then one row per Bought Server." />

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Server** | Its name, with its Location (city and country) under it. |
| **Stage** | Where it is: see the table below. |
| **Size** | The family and size, such as Performance · 4 vCPU · 8 GB RAM · 160 GB disk. |
| **Backups** | **On** or **Off**. |
| **Monthly** | What it costs a month on your plan's invoice. A Deleting Server reads **Not charged**. |
| | **Manage** opens its [Bought Server tab](#the-bought-server-tab); a Server waiting for payment offers **Finish paying** instead. |

| Stage | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Waiting for payment** | Bought, but the payment is not finished yet. Not on the invoice. |
| **Creating** | The machine is being made. It has no Server to open yet. |
| **Setting up** | The machine exists and its setup is running. Nothing can deploy to it yet. |
| **Ready** | It takes Services, and every change is possible. |
| **Changing** | A size, backups, storage or Restore change is running. Deploys to it are refused until it is Ready again. |
| **Deleting** | It is being removed, and is already off the invoice. |

A Server whose machine is deleted, or whose purchase failed, is not listed. The page reads the list
again by itself while a row is Creating, Setting up, Changing or Deleting.

## The Bought Server tab

Every Bought Server has a **Bought Server** tab among its Server tabs, between **Shell** and
**Settings**: eight tabs in all. A Server you added over SSH has no such tab. Its **Settings** tab has
one **Bought Server** line with **Open Bought Server**, and the emails about a change or a Restore link
here.

<Screenshot name="bought-server-tab" alt="The Bought Server tab with Back up now in the header, the Server backups card with Backups on and a list of Server backups with their dates, sizes and Restore buttons, the Size and storage card, and What you pay and About this Server beside them" caption="Server backups first, then size and storage; what it costs and where it is, beside them." />

| Card | What it has |
|---|---|
| **Server backups** | The **Backups** switch, with its price, and while backups are on the list of Server backups with **Restore** on each. **Back up now** is in the tab's header. |
| **Size and storage** | **Size** with **Change size**, and **Extra storage** with **Grow** when the Server was bought with some. |
| **What you pay** | Each line it is charged for (**Server**, **Backups**, **Extra storage**) and the **Total**, on your plan's invoice, monthly in advance. |
| **About this Server** | Its **Location**, **Server image** and **Size**. |

Every change asks first and says what it costs. While one runs, the header shows **Changing** and the
other controls wait with "This Server can be changed once it is Ready."

## Change size, backups or storage

On the **Bought Server** tab, once the Server is Ready:

- **Backups**: the switch asks first. Turning on (**Pay and turn on backups**) charges the rest of this month now. Turning off (**Turn off and delete backups**) deletes every backup at once and credits the unused part of the month. If the Server's size is no longer sold where it is, backups cannot be turned on again.
- **Extra storage**: shown when the Server was bought with some. **Grow**, enter a larger size, and press **Pay and grow**. The rest of the month is charged now, and the filesystem is grown for you.
- **Size**: **Change size** lists the other sizes sold where the Server is, with their price. Sizes with a smaller disk than the current one are marked **Disk too small**. A dearer size is **Pay and change size**, charged now for the rest of the month; a cheaper one is credited on the next invoice.

<Callout type="warn" title="What a change costs you">
A size change powers the Server off for a few minutes, and everything on it stops until it is back.
Its disk is not grown with the size; for more disk, grow extra storage. Turning off backups deletes them
for good, and extra storage is never in the backups: back it up with
[volume backups](/docs/guide/backups) to a Destination outside the Server.
</Callout>

## What is a Server backup?

A Server backup is a copy of a Bought Server's whole disk, made while its backups are on: one a day,
with the last 7 kept, newest first. It is not a [Volume backup or a database Backup](/docs/guide/backups),
which copy one volume or one database to a Destination of your own; a Server backup stays with the
machine and puts the whole disk back.

While backups are on, the **Server backups** card lists each one with when it was **Taken** (the day and
the minute, in your own time zone) and its **Size**, or **Being made** while it is still being made.
The strip above the list says how many are kept, at most 7, or "The first backup is taken within a
day." right after backups are turned on. With backups off there is no list: turning them off deletes
every backup.

### How do I back up a Bought Server now?

Press **Back up now** in the tab's header and confirm in **Back up fra-1 now?**. One more backup is made
at once, at no extra cost, in the same 7 slots as the daily ones. Once all 7 are full, the confirmation
names the one it pushes out, such as "All 7 slots are full, so Tuesday 03:00 will be pushed out." The
new backup shows as **Being made** until it is whole.

**Back up now** is shown only while backups are on. It is refused while the Server is not Ready and
while the Organization is frozen. It works during maintenance and with a deletion date set, since it
costs nothing.

## How do I restore a Bought Server from a backup?

Press **Restore** on the backup's row, read what the dialog says will be lost, type the Server's name
and press **Restore this Server**. The disk goes back to that backup, and every Service deployed after
it is redeployed:

<Steps>
<Step title="Pick the backup">
On the **Bought Server** tab, under **Server backups**, press **Restore** on the row of the backup to go
back to. A backup still **Being made** cannot be restored yet.
</Step>
<Step title="Read the dialog">
**Restore fra-1 to** the backup's date and time says that everything written to the disk since then is
lost, that the Server is offline while it is restored, usually for a few minutes, and that Server setup
then runs on it again. With extra storage, it says that `/mnt/storage` is not in a backup, so it is not
restored and keeps what it holds now.

<Screenshot name="server-backup-restore" alt="The Restore a Server backup dialog naming the backup's date, saying everything written since then is lost, that the Server is offline for a few minutes and Server setup runs again, that a safety copy is kept first, and a field to type the Server's name" caption="Type the Server's name to restore; a safety copy of the disk as it is now is kept first." />
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm">
Type the Server's name and press **Restore this Server**. The Server shows **Changing**, and the strip
reads "Restoring the backup of" the date, until Server setup has run on it again.
</Step>
</Steps>

A Restore then runs these steps by itself, and picks up where it was if Cloudgate restarts:

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Safety copy** | A backup of the disk as it is now, kept as one more Server backup, so you can undo the Restore by restoring it. |
| **Put the disk back** | The disk is rebuilt from the backup you chose. |
| **Start** | The Server is powered on again. |
| **Server setup** | Setup runs again on the restored disk, and mounts the extra storage again. |
| **Redeploy** | The Server is Ready. Every Service on it whose current Deployment finished after the backup is redeployed, so it runs its current version on the restored data. |
| **Email** | The owners and admins get one email naming the backup, how setup ended and each Service redeployed or not, with a link to the Bought Server tab. |

"Current Deployment" is an Application's or a Compose stack's newest finished Deployment (not a
preview's or a Cron service's run), and a database's or a Bucket's last deploy. When an Application's
newest change after the backup was a rollback, it is rolled back to that version again instead. A
Service that cannot be redeployed shows as failed on its own page, and the email says why.

<Callout type="warn" title="A Restore cannot be undone without its safety copy">
Everything written to the disk since the backup is lost. The safety copy is the only way back. When the
backup you chose is the oldest of 7, a safety copy would push it out, so none can be taken: the dialog
says so and asks you to tick **Restore without a safety copy** as well. Copy out anything you need
first.
</Callout>

A Restore is never charged, and a deletion date does not stop it. If it cannot be done, the owners get
an email saying whether the Server is as it was, and what to do next: try again from the Bought Server
tab, or press **Run setup** on its Setup card when the disk was already put back.

### When is Restore refused?

| You see | Why |
|---|---|
| **This Server can be changed once it is Ready.** | Another change, or another Restore, is running, or the Server is not set up yet. |
| **This Server can be restored once its maintenance is ended.** | The Server is [in maintenance](/docs/guide/servers#put-a-server-in-maintenance). |
| **This backup can be restored once it is made.** | The backup is still **Being made**. |
| **Your role in this Organization cannot back up or restore this Server.** | Restore and Back up now need the owner or admin role. |
| **This Server was powered off by an operator, so it can't be changed** | An operator of the Hosted instance powered it off. |
| A frozen Organization's refusal | The Organization is [frozen](/docs/guide/organizations#when-an-organization-is-frozen). |

## Delete a Bought Server

On **Settings**, under **Remove this Server**, press **Delete**, type the Server's name and press
**Delete this Server**. The machine is deleted with its disk, every backup and its extra storage, and
its address is released. The unused part of the month is credited on your next invoice. Removal is
refused while Services are still recorded on the Server; move or delete them first.

<Callout type="warn" title="This cannot be undone">
Unlike a Server you added over SSH, deleting a Bought Server destroys the machine and everything on it,
its Server backups included. Copy out what you need first.
</Callout>

## Notices on a Bought Server

| Notice | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Due for deletion on** a date | A payment failed. The Server keeps working until then. | **Pay the open invoice** in Settings, Billing. |
| **Powered off by an operator** | An operator of the Hosted instance powered it off, with the reason shown. | Reply to the email about it, or write to support@octabyte.io. |
| **Deleted.** | Its machine was deleted; its Services are still listed. | Move or delete the Services, then delete the Server. |

While a Server is powered off by an operator, a deploy, a rollback, a Start or restart, a Run now, an
upload and a preview are refused with a message naming the Server, a Template setup waits, and pickers
leave it out.

## If a payment fails

A failed payment sets a deletion date 7 days later for the Organization's Bought Servers, and the red
bar shows it on each. A suspension of the Organization, or a cancel that is coming, sets one too. While
a deletion date is set, buying and anything that costs more (backups on, more storage, a bigger size)
are refused; deleting, turning backups off, moving to a smaller size, **Back up now** and **Restore**
stay allowed.

## What you cannot do

- Buy without being charged: there is no trial for a Bought Server.
- Pay with a prepaid card: the purchase is refused and nothing is charged.
- Keep more than 7 Server backups, or keep them after turning backups off.
- Restore a Server backup onto another Server, or restore only some files from it.
- Shrink extra storage, include it in the backups, or add it later to a Server bought without it.
- Change to a size not sold in the Server's location.
