A bought Server is a Server Octabyte runs for you. Once it is ready it works like any other
[Server](/docs/guide/servers): you deploy Applications, databases and Compose stacks onto it, open its
shell and read its telemetry. Cloudgate makes its key and runs its setup by itself.

## What can you do with a bought Server?

- Buy one by location, size and image, from a world map, with a price before you pay.
- Add daily backups of the Server's disk, and extra storage from 10 GB to 10 TB.
- Change its size up or down, turn backups on or off, and grow its extra storage later.
- Delete it, with the unused part of the month credited.

<Callout type="limit" title="Hosted only, owners and admins">
**Buy a Server** shows only on the Hosted instance, at cloudgate.octabyte.app. On a self-hosted
instance, [add your own Server over SSH](/docs/guide/servers). Buying and changing a bought Server
needs the owner or admin role, or a custom role that allows it; the button is greyed out for others.
</Callout>

## How do I buy a Server?

Press **Buy a Server**, pick a location, a size and an image, add any extras, and pay. Cloudgate then
creates the machine and runs its setup for you:

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Buy a Server">
In **Servers**, press **Buy a Server**. The [get-started wizard](/docs/guide/get-started) offers the
same choices in its first step.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick a location">
**Suggested** offers **Closest** and **Cheapest**: the least a Server with at least 4 GB of memory costs
near you, and anywhere. Or pick under **1. Location**: a pin on the map or a city card, nearest first,
each with its distance. Your position is first guessed from your browser's time zone; **Show where I
am** asks your browser for its location, and it stays on the page.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick a size">
Under **2. Size in** the chosen city, choose a family, then a size. Each row gives vCPUs, memory, disk
and the price per month. A size not sold there right now is greyed out.

| Family | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Standard** | Shared Intel or AMD cores, the cheapest, enough for most apps. |
| **Performance** | Shared AMD cores, faster than Standard, in more locations. |
| **Arm** | Shared Arm cores, the cheapest per core. Your images must run on Arm. |
| **Dedicated** | Cores reserved for this Server alone, for steady heavy load. |
</Step>
<Step title="Pick the image">
**3. Server image** lists only the images the size's architecture can run, and starts on Ubuntu 24.04.
</Step>
<Step title="Add extras">
Under **4. Extras**:

| Extra | What it is | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Backups** | A copy of the Server's own disk every day; the last 7 are kept. | Priced once you pick a size. Extra storage is not included. |
| **Extra storage** | A separate disk, in GB, mounted at `/mnt/storage`. | 10 GB to 10 TB. Can grow later, never shrink. Leave empty for none. |
</Step>
<Step title="Check the Summary and buy">
The **Summary** lists the Server, backups and extra storage with their monthly prices, your plan, and
the **Total per month**. **Today** is what is charged now, for the part of the month up to your plan's
renewal. Tick that you accept Octabyte's terms and acceptable-use policy, then press **Buy**. If the
Organization has no plan yet, the button reads **Buy and start Free trial** and starts the trial with
the purchase. The line under the button says what is still missing.

</Step>
<Step title="Wait until it is ready">
When a payment page is needed you are sent there and back; your bank may ask you to confirm. The
Server is then created and set up by itself. Its **Setup** card reads **Setting up**, and nothing can
deploy to it until setup finishes. The first setup waits up to ten minutes for the new machine to accept
SSH.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note" title="When your Server count is full">
If the Organization already has all the Servers its plan allows, the Summary adds an Add-on line ("room
for 1 more Server") and the Add-on is bought with the Server. See [Billing](/docs/guide/billing).
</Callout>

### Finish or cancel an unpaid purchase

A purchase left at the payment step shows in **Servers being bought** on the Servers screen as
**Waiting for payment**. **Finish paying** returns you to the payment; **Cancel** drops it and nothing is
charged. Rows also show **Creating** and, while one is removed, **Deleting**. If creating the machine
fails, what you paid is given back in full.

## Change size, backups or storage

Open the Server's **Settings** tab and its **Bought Server** card, which shows its monthly price. Changes
are possible once the Server is ready; a **Changing** chip shows while one runs.

- **Backups**: the switch asks first. Turning on (**Pay and turn on backups**) charges the rest of this month now. Turning off (**Turn off and delete backups**) deletes every backup at once and credits the unused part of the month.
- **Extra storage**: shown when the Server was bought with some. **Grow**, enter a larger size, and press **Pay and grow**. The filesystem is grown for you.
- **Size**: **Change size** lists the other sizes sold where the Server is, with their price. Sizes with a smaller disk than the current one are marked **Disk too small**. A dearer size is **Pay and change size**, charged now for the rest of the month; a cheaper one is credited on the next invoice.

<Callout type="warn" title="What a change costs you">
A size change powers the Server off for a few minutes, and everything on it stops until it is back.
Its disk is not grown with the size; for more disk, grow extra storage. Turning off backups deletes them
for good, and extra storage is never in the backups: back it up with
[volume backups](/docs/guide/backups) to a Destination outside the Server.
</Callout>

## Delete a bought Server

On **Settings**, under **Remove this Server**, press **Delete**, type the Server's name and press
**Delete this Server**. The machine is deleted with its disk, every backup and its extra storage, and
its address is released. The unused part of the month is credited on your next invoice. Removal is
refused while Services are still recorded on the Server; move or delete them first.

<Callout type="warn" title="This cannot be undone">
Unlike a Server you added over SSH, deleting a bought Server destroys the machine and everything on it.
Copy out what you need first.
</Callout>

## Notices on a bought Server

| Notice | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Due for deletion on** a date | A payment failed. The Server keeps working until then. | **Pay the open invoice** in Settings, Billing. |
| **Powered off by an operator** | An operator of the Hosted instance powered it off, with the reason shown. | Reply to the email about it, or write to support@octabyte.io. |
| **Deleted.** | Its machine was deleted; its Services are still listed. | Move or delete the Services, then delete the Server. |

While a Server is powered off by an operator, a deploy, a rollback, a Start or restart, a Run now, an
upload and a preview are refused with a message naming the Server, a Template setup waits, and pickers
leave it out.

## If a payment fails

A failed payment sets a deletion date 7 days later for the Organization's bought Servers, and the red
bar shows it on each. A suspension of the Organization, or a cancel that is coming, sets one too. While
a deletion date is set, buying and anything that costs more (backups on, more storage, a bigger size)
are refused; deleting, turning backups off and moving to a smaller size stay allowed.

## What you cannot do

- Buy without being charged: there is no trial for a bought Server.
- Pay with a prepaid card: the purchase is refused and nothing is charged.
- Shrink extra storage, include it in the backups, or add it later to a Server bought without it.
- Change to a size not sold in the Server's location.
