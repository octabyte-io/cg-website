A Bought Server is a Server Octabyte owns and runs for you. Once it is ready it works like any
other [Server](/docs/guide/servers): you deploy Applications, databases and Compose stacks onto it.
You do not need to make an SSH key or run the setup: Cloudgate does both.

## What you can do

- Buy a Server by location and size, from a world map.
- Add backups of the Server, and extra storage from 10 GB to 10 TB.
- Change its size up or down, turn backups on or off, and grow its storage later.
- Delete it, with the unused part of the month credited.

## Buy a Server

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the shop">
In **Servers**, press **Buy a Server**. You can also get here from the
[get-started wizard](/docs/guide/get-started) by choosing **Get a server from Cloudgate**.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick a location">
Choose a location on the world map. **Show where I am** highlights the ones near you, using your
browser's time zone; it is never sent anywhere.
<Screenshot name="servers-buy" alt="The Buy a Server page with a world map of locations, a list of sizes and a price summary" caption="Pick a location, then a size; sizes not sold there are greyed out." />
</Step>
<Step title="Pick a size and extras">
Choose a size sold in that location; sizes not sold there are greyed out. Optionally turn on
backups and add extra storage, from 10 GB to 10 TB.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the summary and buy">
The summary shows the monthly price, today's charge and the dates. Tick that you accept Octabyte's
terms and acceptable-use policy, then press **Buy**. The first time, your bank may ask you to
confirm the payment (3D Secure).
</Step>
<Step title="Wait until it is ready">
The Server moves through **Waiting for payment**, **Creating**, **Setting up** and **Ready**. Its
first setup run starts by itself. Nothing deploys onto it until it is ready.
</Step>
</Steps>

If a purchase is left unpaid, **Finish paying** returns you to the payment, or you can cancel it.
If creating the machine fails, you are refunded.

## Change size, backups or storage

Open the Server's **Settings** tab, **Bought Server** card.

- **Change size**: move up or down among the sizes sold in its location (**Pay and change size**).
  The machine is powered off for a few minutes. The disk is not grown.
- **Extra storage**: grow it (**Pay and grow**). The filesystem is grown for you. Extra storage is
  not in the backups.
- **Backups**: **Pay and turn on backups**, or turn them off.

A costlier change is charged now; a cheaper one is credited on the next invoice.

<Callout type="warn" title="Turning off backups deletes them">
**Turn off and delete backups** deletes every backup of the Server at once. Extra storage can only
grow, never shrink.
</Callout>

## Delete a bought Server

Removing it from the Server's **Settings** deletes the machine, its data and its backups. The
unused part of the month is credited.

<Callout type="warn" title="This cannot be undone">
Unlike a Server you added over SSH, deleting a bought Server destroys the machine and everything on
it. Back up the databases and volumes you need first; see [Backups](/docs/guide/backups).
</Callout>

## If a payment fails

- A failed invoice sets a deletion date 7 days later; so does a suspension. A red "Due for
  deletion on" bar shows the date. Costlier changes are refused until it is paid.
- When the plan lapses, bought Servers are deleted at once.
- A Server deleted for non-payment credits nothing. Its record stays, marked Deleted.

## What you cannot do

<Callout type="limit" title="Hosted only">
Buying a Server is offered only on the Hosted instance, at cloudgate.octabyte.app. On a
self-hosted instance, [add your own Server over SSH](/docs/guide/servers).
</Callout>

- Buy without being charged: there is no trial for a bought Server.
- Pay with a prepaid card.
- Shrink extra storage, or include it in the backups.
- Change the size to one not sold in the Server's location.
