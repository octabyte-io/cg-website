<Callout type="limit" title="Hosted instance only">
Billing exists only on the Hosted instance at cloudgate.octabyte.app, and only owners and admins
can open it. On a self-hosted instance, **Settings** > **Billing** says "Billing is not used on
this instance": it is free to use under the Elastic License 2.0. See
[Self-hosting](/docs/self-host).
</Callout>

## What you can do

- Start a {trialDays}-day free trial of Starter or Business, or subscribe straight away.
- Upgrade to Business, move back to Starter, or switch plan during the trial.
- Buy or remove Add-ons for more Servers and Cloud Environments.
- Cancel a trial, change the card, and download invoices.
- On Business, pay for other Organizations from the same plan.

## Plans

| | {plans[0].name} | {plans[1].name} |
|---|---|---|
| Price | {plans[0].price} USD a month | {plans[1].price} USD a month |
| Servers | 3 | 3 |
| Cloud Environments | 3 | 3 |
| Members, Projects, Environments | not counted | not counted |
| SSO, SCIM, custom roles, forward auth | not included | included |
| Pay for more Organizations on one plan | not included | included |

An **Add-on** is {addOn.price} USD a month on either plan and adds {addOn.what}. Plans are
monthly only. A **Cloud Environment** is an Environment that deploys to a Cloud account; it counts
once however many accounts or regions it uses, and connecting a Cloud account counts nothing.
Enterprise is billed by agreement: contact support@octabyte.io. Full prices are on
[Pricing](/pricing).

## The Billing screen

**Settings** > **Billing** (`/settings/billing`) has four cards: **Current plan**, **Plans**,
**Organizations on this plan** and the invoice cards.

<Screenshot name="billing" alt="Settings, Billing for Northwind Labs on an active Business plan with 2 Add-ons: 5 Servers and 5 Cloud Environments allowed, meters at 3 of 5 Servers and 1 of 5 Cloud Environments in use, and the Plans card with Starter and Business, the Add-ons buttons, Update Add-ons and Move to Starter" caption="Current plan shows what the Organization may hold and how much is in use; Plans changes it." />

### Current plan

| Row | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Plan** | Starter, Business or Enterprise, with **Free trial · N days left**, **Moves to Starter on ...** when a move is scheduled, or **Not active** when the plan has ended. |
| **Status** | Stripe's own word for the subscription, such as `trialing`, `active` or `past_due`, or **No subscription**. |
| **Trial ends** | During a trial, its last day. |
| **First charge** | During a trial: the amount, the day and the card, such as "on ..., to the Visa ending 4242 (Business + 1 Add-on)". After **Cancel trial** it reads "No plan. Nothing is charged." |
| **Add-ons** | How many the subscription holds. |
| **Servers allowed**, **Cloud Environments allowed** | The plan's 3 each, plus one per Add-on. |

Below them, a meter for **Servers in use** and **Cloud Environments in use**. At the count it says
adding another is refused until an Add-on makes room; over it, that everything keeps running. With
other Organizations on the plan, it says how many are used in this one and how many in the others.

- **Manage billing at Stripe** opens Stripe's billing portal, where you change the card. It is shown
  once the Organization has been through Stripe Checkout.
- **Add a card at Stripe** appears during a trial with no card on file for the first charge.
- **Cancel trial** and **Keep ...** are described under [Cancel the free
  trial](#cancel-the-free-trial).

With no plan, the card says what is needed: "Choose a plan to add Servers and Cloud Environments.
Projects, members, Git connections and Cloud accounts need no plan."

## Start the free trial

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Billing">
Open **Settings** > **Billing**.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick a plan and Add-ons">
Under **Plans**, choose **Starter** or **Business** (Business is picked to begin with). Each shows
its price, "3 Servers and 3 Cloud Environments", "More with Add-ons" and what else it includes.
Use the **Add-ons** buttons (minus and plus) if you need more than three Servers; the line under
them says how many Servers and Cloud Environments that allows.

</Step>
<Step title="Start it">
The terms read "Free for 30 days, then from ...", with the monthly total. Press **Start 30-day
free trial**. You go to Stripe Checkout; nothing changes in Cloudgate until it goes through.
</Step>
<Step title="Add a card">
Enter a card at Stripe. The trial counts as paid for the plan's counts, and nothing is charged
until it ends.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="limit" title="One trial each">
A card is needed to start the trial. There is one trial per Organization, per person and per card.
If you are not eligible, the button reads **Subscribe** and you pay today ("Today, then every
month"). An Organization paid for by another one never gets a trial.
</Callout>

## Cancel the free trial

Press **Cancel trial** under **Current plan**. "Cancel the free trial?" says the plan stays until
the trial's last day and then there is no plan and nothing is charged. Press **Cancel trial** to
confirm, or **Back**. Until that day, **Keep Starter** or **Keep Business** takes it back.

## Change plan

| Button | Shown when | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Upgrade to Business** | On an active Starter subscription. | "Upgrade to Business?" shows today's amount for the days left of this month and the monthly price after. Stripe charges the card now; if the payment fails, the Organization stays on Starter. |
| **Move to Starter** | On an active Business subscription. | "Move to Starter?": Business stays until the end of the month you paid for, then Starter, with the same Servers, Cloud Environments and Add-ons. Nothing is charged now. The dialog lists what stops, then **Downgrade on ...**. |
| **Switch to Business**, **Switch to Starter** | During the trial. | The new plan starts now; the trial still ends on the same day, and the first charge is for whatever you hold then. |
| **Keep Business** | While a move to Starter is waiting. | Cancels the move. Nothing is charged. |
| **Update Add-ons** | On any held plan, after changing the Add-ons. | Changes the subscription; Stripe prorates the difference. |

Before a move to Starter, the dialog lists what stops: SSO sign-in (people who sign in only with
SSO will use **Forgot password**), SCIM adds (removals still work), custom roles (allowing
nothing, naming who holds one) and changes to forward auth (protected Applications stay
protected). All these settings are kept and work again on Business.

You cannot hold fewer Add-ons than you use: if the Organization already uses Servers beyond the
plan's three, the minus button stops there and the line says why.

<Callout type="note" title="Moving to Starter with other Organizations on the plan">
Starter pays for one Organization. If your Business plan pays for others, **Before ... can move
to Starter** lists them first: press **Stop paying for it** on each, or delete one you own. The
move waits until none is left.
</Callout>

## What happens at your limit

When the Organization has all the Servers or Cloud Environments its plan allows, only the next one
is refused. Everything already running keeps running. Where you add a Server, a notice says
"This Organization has all the Servers its plan allows" with **Buy an Add-on**. Without any plan,
adding a Server or a Cloud Environment is refused ("This Organization has no plan, so a Server
cannot be added yet", with **Choose a plan**); Projects, members, Git connections and Cloud
accounts need no plan.

## Bought Servers

A Bought Server is a Server you buy from Cloudgate in the console. It is billed on top of the plan,
monthly in advance, on the same invoice, at the price shown before you buy. It counts as one of the
plan's Servers.

<Callout type="warn" title="Charged at once, deleted when unpaid">
A Bought Server is charged immediately, even during a free trial. If its payment fails or the
Organization is suspended, it is deleted 7 days later; if the Organization's plan lapses, it is
deleted at once, with its data and backups.
</Callout>

More in [Bought Servers](/docs/guide/bought-servers).

## Pay for other Organizations (Business)

**Organizations on this plan** lists the Organizations this plan pays for. Each has its own
members, and they all share the plan's Servers and Cloud Environments.

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Organization** | Its name. |
| **Owner** | Its owner's name and email. |
| **Servers**, **Cloud Environments** | How many it uses from the shared counts. |
| **Made** | When it was made. |

- **Add an Organization to this plan** opens a dialog that says how many Servers and Cloud
  Environments are free on the plan. Type a **Name** and press **Add Organization**. You are its
  owner and only member, and you stay where you are. On Starter, or with no plan, the button is off
  and a line says why, for example "... is on Starter, which pays for one Organization."
- **Stop paying for it** asks "Stop paying for ...?". It freezes at once: its Services keep
  running, but nothing new starts. It is deleted after 30 days unless its owner pays for it, and
  its owner is emailed. Any Bought Server it has is deleted at once, with its data and backups.
  Press **Stop paying**.

### When another Organization pays for yours

If your Organization is on someone else's plan, **Current plan** shows **Paid by ...**, the
Servers and Cloud Environments in this Organization, and how many are left on the plan (shared
with the other Organizations on it). Only that Organization's owners and admins change the plan.
**Pay for it yourself** takes your Organization off their plan: you pick a plan and pay, with no
free trial, and it stays on their plan until your first payment goes through.

## Invoices

- **Invoice emails**: the **Email me about invoices** switch sends invoice emails to the
  Organization owner's address (the one Stripe bills), not to whoever turns it on.
- **Invoices**: every invoice from Stripe, newest first, with **Invoice** number, **Issued**,
  **Amount**, **Status** and a **PDF** link. "No invoices" until something has been billed.

## If a payment fails

A banner on every page says **A payment failed.** Stripe will retry it; nothing has stopped yet.
Update the card with **Manage billing at Stripe**. If Stripe stops retrying, the plan lapses and
the Organization is frozen: everything keeps running, but nothing new starts, and it is deleted 30
days later unless you pay. See [Organizations](/docs/guide/organizations#when-an-organization-is-frozen).

## What you cannot do

<Callout type="limit" title="Limits">
- Billing is for owners and admins; for anyone else the page says it cannot be read.
- Plans are monthly; there is no yearly price.
- A person can own one Organization that pays for itself.
- An Organization paid for by another one never gets a free trial.
- On Enterprise there is no plan to choose: Octabyte raises the counts.
</Callout>
