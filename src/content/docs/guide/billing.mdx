<Callout type="limit" title="Hosted instance only">
Billing exists only on the Hosted instance at cloudgate.octabyte.app. A self-hosted instance is
free to use under the Elastic License 2.0; see [Self-hosting](/docs/self-host).
</Callout>

## What you can do

- Start a {trialDays}-day free trial of Starter or Business.
- Subscribe, upgrade to Business, or move back to Starter.
- Buy Add-ons for more Servers and Cloud Environments.
- Download invoices and choose who is emailed about them.
- On Business, pay for other Organizations from the same plan.

## Plans

| | {plans[0].name} | {plans[1].name} |
|---|---|---|
| Price | {plans[0].price} USD a month | {plans[1].price} USD a month |
| Servers | 3 | 3 |
| Cloud Environments | 3 | 3 |
| Members, Projects, Environments | not counted | not counted |
| SSO, SCIM, custom roles, forward auth | not included | included |
| Pay for more Organizations on one plan | not included | included |

An **Add-on** is {addOn.price} USD a month on either plan and adds {addOn.what}. Plans are
monthly only. A **Cloud Environment** is an Environment that deploys to a Cloud account; it counts
once however many accounts or regions it uses, and connecting a Cloud account counts nothing.
Enterprise is billed by agreement: contact support@octabyte.io. Full prices are on
[Pricing](/pricing).

## Start the free trial

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Billing">
Open **Settings** > **Billing**. **Current plan** shows your plan, status, the Servers and Cloud
Environments allowed, your Add-ons and when a trial ends.
<Screenshot name="billing" alt="Settings, Billing for Northwind Labs on a Starter free trial, with Servers allowed, Cloud Environments allowed and the trial end date" caption="Current plan shows what the Organization may add and when the trial ends." />
</Step>
<Step title="Pick a plan">
Under **Plans**, choose Starter or Business and press **Start 30-day free trial**.
</Step>
<Step title="Add a card">
Enter a card in Stripe Checkout. The trial counts as paid for the plan's counts, and Add-ons cost
nothing until it ends.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="limit" title="One trial each">
A card is needed to start the trial. There is one trial per Organization, per person and per card;
anyone not eligible subscribes and pays today. During the trial you can switch between Starter and
Business while the clock keeps running. **Cancel trial** ends it at the trial's last day with
nothing charged.
</Callout>

## Change plan

- **Upgrade to Business** takes effect at once and is prorated.
- **Move to Starter** keeps Business until the end of the month you paid for, then moves to
  Starter with the same Servers, Cloud Environments and Add-ons. Nothing is charged now. **Keep
  Business** takes it back while it waits.
- **Update Add-ons** adds or removes Add-ons. You cannot hold fewer than you already use.
- **Manage billing at Stripe** changes the card; **Add a card at Stripe** adds one during a trial
  with none on file.

## What happens at your limit

When the Organization has all the Servers or Cloud Environments its plan allows, only the next one
is refused. Everything already running keeps running. Buy an Add-on or upgrade, then add it again.
Without any plan, adding a Server or a Cloud Environment is refused; Projects, members, Git
connections and Cloud accounts need no plan.

## Bought Servers

A Bought Server is a Server you buy from Cloudgate in the console. It is billed on top of the plan,
monthly in advance, on the same invoice, at the price shown before you buy. It counts as one of the
plan's Servers.

<Callout type="warn" title="Charged at once, deleted when unpaid">
A Bought Server is charged immediately, even during a free trial. If its payment fails or the
Organization is suspended, it is deleted 7 days later; if the Organization's plan lapses, it is
deleted at once, with its data and backups.
</Callout>

More in [Bought Servers](/docs/guide/bought-servers).

## Pay for other Organizations (Business)

On Business, **Organizations on this plan** > **Add an Organization to this plan** makes a new
Organization this plan pays for. Each has its own members, and they all share the plan's Servers
and Cloud Environments. **Stop paying for it** makes it lapse. Its owner can choose **Pay for it
yourself** instead.

## Invoices

**Invoices** lists every invoice from Stripe, newest first, as a PDF. **Email me about invoices**
sends them to the Organization owner's address.

## If a payment fails

Stripe retries a failed payment. If it stops retrying, the plan lapses and the Organization is
frozen: everything keeps running, but nothing new starts, and it is deleted 30 days later unless
you pay. See [Organizations](/docs/guide/organizations#when-an-organization-is-frozen).

## What you cannot do

<Callout type="limit" title="Limits">
- Billing is for owners and admins of an Organization that pays for itself.
- Plans are monthly; there is no yearly price.
- A person can own one Organization that pays for itself.
- An Attached Organization never gets a free trial.
</Callout>
