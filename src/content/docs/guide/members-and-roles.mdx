Every Organization has exactly one owner. Everyone else is an admin, a member, or holds a custom
role. Members see only what you grant them; owners and admins see everything.

## What you can do

- Invite people by email and resend or revoke an invitation.
- Change a member's role, or remove them.
- Limit a member to certain Projects, Environments and Services.
- Group people in Teams.
- Build custom roles from the same permission table the backend checks (paid).

## The built-in roles

| Role | What it can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything, including deleting the Organization. One per Organization; cannot be transferred. |
| **Admin** | Everything except deleting the Organization. Reaches every Project. |
| **Member** | Reads and updates the Services and Environments they are granted; deploys, cancels, backs up and restores them; adds Domains. Cannot create or delete a Project, an Environment or a Service, add Servers, or manage members. |

Settings > **Members** has a **What each role can do** grid with the full list.

## Invite someone

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Members">
Open **Settings** > **Members** and press **Invite someone**.
<Screenshot name="members" alt="Settings, Members for Northwind Labs, listing people with their role and member-since date, and pending invitations" caption="Each row has Change role, Manage access and Remove." />
</Step>
<Step title="Enter their details">
Enter the **Email** they will sign in with, choose a **Role** (Member, Admin, or a custom role),
and optionally a **Team**. Press **Create invitation**.

<Screenshot name="members-invite" alt="The Invite someone dialog on the Members screen of Northwind Labs" caption="An invitation names the person's email address and the role they join with." />

</Step>
<Step title="Send them the link">
The dialog shows **Send them this link**. Copy the invitation link and pass it on. If the instance
has no mail sender, nothing was emailed and sending it is your job. The link stays in
**Invitations** if you close the dialog.
</Step>
</Steps>

They sign in, or create an account, and accept. An invitation expires after 48 hours; **Resend** it
or revoke it from **Invitations**.

## Grant access to Projects, Environments and Services

A member reaches nothing until you grant it. On their row, press **Manage access** and tick what
they may reach. A member reaches a Service through its Project and its Environment, so granting a
Service grants the two above it. For example, grant `storefront` / `staging` / `web` to let someone
work on staging without seeing production.

Admins and the owner reach everything, so grants are not checked while someone holds those roles.

## Change a role or remove a member

- **Change role**: takes effect on their next request; they do not have to sign in again. You cannot
  change your own role or the owner's.
- **Remove**: their account stays, their membership of this Organization goes. A session they have
  open stops being allowed to do anything on its next request.

## Create a custom role

<Callout type="limit" title="Business plan or Enterprise license">
Custom roles need the Business plan on the Hosted instance, or an Enterprise license on a
self-hosted instance. Without one, a custom role allows nothing, and people holding it can sign in
but do nothing until you give them a built-in role.
</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Roles">
Open **Settings** > **Roles** and press **Create a role**.

<Screenshot name="roles" alt="The Roles screen with the custom roles release-manager and on-call and the permissions each allows" caption="Custom roles sit beside the built-in ones." />

</Step>
<Step title="Name it and tick what it allows">
Give it a lower-case name, such as `deployer`, and tick the subjects and actions it allows. Every
choice comes from the same permission table the backend's guards check.
</Step>
<Step title="Assign it">
Save, then pick it in **Change role** on a member's row, or when you invite someone.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What you cannot do

<Callout type="limit" title="Limits">
- The owner role cannot be transferred.
- At most ten custom roles per Organization.
- Teams group people only; they do not change what anyone can do.
- On a self-hosted instance, only the first sign-up arrives without an invitation.
- A member deactivated over SCIM cannot sign in; only the directory can reactivate them.
- Agents are managed under Settings > **Agents**, not here.
</Callout>
