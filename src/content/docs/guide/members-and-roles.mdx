Every Organization has exactly one owner. Everyone else is an admin, a member, or holds a custom
role. A role says what someone may **do**; their access grants say what they may **reach**. Owners
and admins reach everything, so grants matter only for members and custom roles.

Members are managed at **Settings** > **Members** (`/settings/members`) and custom roles at
**Settings** > **Roles** (`/settings/roles`). Agents are members too, but they are managed under
[Agents](/docs/guide/agents), not here.

## What you can do

- Invite people by email, copy their invitation link, and revoke an invitation.
- Change a member's role, or remove them from the Organization.
- Grant a member access to certain Projects, Environments and Services.
- Read exactly what each role allows, from the permission table Cloudgate checks.
- Group people in Teams.
- Build up to ten custom roles (Business plan or Enterprise license).

## What can each built-in role do?

The three built-in roles are defined by Cloudgate itself, so nobody can change what they mean.

| Role | What it can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything, including deleting the Organization. One per Organization; it cannot be given away or taken. |
| **Admin** | Everything except deleting the Organization. Reaches every Project, Environment and Service. |
| **Member** | On the Services and Environments granted to them: read and update them, deploy, cancel a Deployment, back up and restore databases and volumes, manage backup schedules and volumes, add and delete Domains, read monitoring and tags. Cannot create or delete a Project, Environment or Service, add Servers, registries, Cloud accounts, certificates, Destinations or notification channels, connect Git providers, read the audit log, or manage members. |

The **What each role can do** card on the Members screen shows the full list for every role.

## The Members screen

<Screenshot name="members" alt="Settings, Members for Northwind Labs, listing four people with their role and member-since date, and a pending invitation for eli@northwind.example" caption="Each row has Change role, Manage access and Remove; the owner's row and your own say why they have none." />

The screen has four cards, top to bottom: **Members**, **Invitations**, **What each role can do**
and **Teams**. A banner can appear above them (see [Locked out by a custom
role](#what-does-locked-out-by-a-custom-role-mean)).

### Members

Lists the people in this Organization, with a count such as "4 people in this Organization."

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Person** | Name and email address. **You** marks your own row. A **deactivated** mark and a line under the address appear when the person cannot sign in (see [SCIM](#what-does-deactivated-mean)). |
| **Role** | `owner`, `admin`, `member` or a custom role's name. |
| **Member since** | When they joined this Organization (not when their account was made). |
| Actions | **Change role**, **Manage access** and **Remove**. The owner's row says **The owner cannot be changed**; your own row says **You cannot change your own role**. |

Agents are not listed. When the Organization has any, the card's footer says how many and links to
**Agents**.

If your role cannot use a button, it is greyed out and its tooltip says so, for example "Your role
in this Organization cannot invite someone. Ask an owner or an admin to do it, or to change your
role."

## Invite someone

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the dialog">
On **Settings** > **Members**, press **Invite someone**. Inviting takes an owner or an admin.
</Step>
<Step title="Fill in the invitation">
<Screenshot name="members-invite" alt="The Invite someone dialog with sam@northwind.example in Email and the Role left on Member" caption="The hint under Role describes the role you picked." />

| Field | What it does | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Email** | The address they will sign in with. | Required. An invitation works only for this address. |
| **Role** | What they join as: **Member**, **Admin**, or one of the Organization's custom roles (each shows how many people hold it). | Defaults to Member. Owner is never offered. |
| **Team** | Adds them to a Team when they accept. | Optional. Shown only when the Organization has Teams. Defaults to **No team**. |

Press **Create invitation**.
</Step>
<Step title="Send them the link">
The dialog changes to **Send them this link** and shows the invitation link, of the form
`https://<console>/accept-invitation/<id>`, with a copy button. Copy it and send it to them, then
press **Done**. The link also stays in **Invitations** while it is waiting.

<Screenshot name="members-invite-link" alt="The Send them this link dialog showing the invitation link for sam@northwind.example with a copy button" caption="The link is the invitation. Copy it now or later from the Invitations list." />
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note" title="Who sends the email?">
The dialog and the Invitations card say the instance sends no email, and you should plan on sending
the link yourself. If the instance's administrator has set up an email sender, the person is also
emailed the same link.
</Callout>

An invitation expires 48 hours after it is made. There is no resend: to send a fresh one,
revoke the old one and invite them again, which makes a new link.

### Custom roles in an invitation

You can invite someone straight onto a custom role. Without a valid Business plan or Enterprise
license, custom roles are left out of the picker and a notice says why: "Custom roles need a
license before they can be assigned." Admin and Member always work.

## What does the invited person see?

Opening the link shows **You have been invited**, with **Sign in to accept** and **Create an
account**. The invitation is for one email address: signing up with another one is refused with
"That invitation was sent to a different email address."

Once signed in with the right address, **Join this Organization** shows the **Organization**,
**Your role** and **Invited address**. **Accept invitation** adds them, and they see **You are
in**. If they are signed in as someone else, **Sign out** at the foot of the panel lets them switch.

## Invitations

Lists everyone who has been asked and has not joined yet.

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Email** | The invited address. |
| **Role** | The role they will join with. |
| **State** | **waiting**, **expired**, **accepted** or **closed**. |
| **Expires** | When the link stops working. |

A **waiting** row has a copy button for its link and **Revoke**. Any other row has **Delete**.

- **Revoke** asks "Revoke the invitation for ...?" The invitation is deleted, so the link you passed
  on stops working, and it cannot be reissued. Press **Revoke it**, or **Keep it** to back out.
- **Delete** removes a used or expired row from the list. Nobody's access changes, and someone who
  accepted stays a member. Press **Delete it**.

Revoking and deleting invitations take an owner or an admin.

## Change someone's role

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Change role">
On their row in **Members**, press **Change role**. The dialog is titled "Change ...'s role".
</Step>
<Step title="Pick the role and read what it allows">
Choose the **Role**. Under it, **What ... is allowed to do** lists every subject and action the role
holds, ticked or crossed.

<Screenshot name="members-change-role" alt="The Change role dialog for Dana Ruiz with the Role picker set to Member and the permission grid below it" caption="The grid under the picker is what the new role allows, read from the permission table Cloudgate checks." />
</Step>
<Step title="Save">
Press **Change role**. It takes effect on their next request; they do not have to sign in again.
</Step>
</Steps>

Cloudgate refuses four changes: your own role, the owner's role, making anyone the owner, and an
admin changing another admin's role. The first two are greyed out on the row; the other two are
refused with a message in the dialog.

## Grant access to Projects, Environments and Services

A member reaches nothing until you grant it, apart from what they create themselves: whoever
creates a Project, Environment or Service is granted it automatically.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Manage access">
On their row, press **Manage access**. The dialog is titled "Manage ...'s access".
</Step>
<Step title="Tick what they may reach">
The dialog has three lists:

- **Projects**: every Project in this Organization.
- **Environments**: the Environments of the Projects you ticked.
- **Services**: what is deployed in the Environments you ticked (Applications, databases and
  Compose stacks).

Ticking travels up and unticking travels down: tick a Service and its Environment and Project are
ticked too; untick a Project and everything under it goes.

<Screenshot name="members-manage-access" alt="The Manage access dialog for Chen Wei with the storefront Project, its staging Environment and the staging web Application ticked" caption="Grant storefront, staging and web to let someone work on staging without seeing production." />
</Step>
<Step title="Save">
Press **Save access**. It saves the whole set at once.
</Step>
</Steps>

- On an **admin**, a notice says none of the grants is checked while they hold that role. The grants
  apply if they are moved to member or to a custom role.
- If a saved grant names something that has been deleted, or sits under something you are unticking,
  the dialog lists it and saving removes it.

<Callout type="note" title="Updating a Project or Environment reaches further">
Updating a Project or an Environment also rewrites its variables, which every Service under it can
reference, including Services the member is not granted. The grid says so on the **Project** and
**Environments** rows.
</Callout>

## Remove a member

Press **Remove** on their row. The dialog "Remove ...?" explains: their account stays, their
membership of this Organization goes, and they lose access to every Project, Environment and
Server in it. Anything they created stays exactly where it is.

A session they already have open is not signed out. It stops being allowed to do anything here on
its next request. Press **Remove member**, or **Keep them** to back out.

## What each role can do

This card answers "what can this role do?" for every role, straight from the permission table the
backend checks. Pick a role (**Member**, **Admin**, **Owner** or a custom role) and the grid shows
each subject, such as **Services**, **Deployment** or **Backup**, with its actions ticked or
crossed.

Notes on some rows:

- **Not checked for the built-in roles.** Owner and admin are allowed this row whatever it shows.
- **Managed by Cloudgate — a custom role cannot be given this.** Membership, invitations, Teams and
  the Organization itself. Only the built-in roles carry these.

The footer repeats that the owner role cannot be given away or taken.

## Teams

Teams group people. They do not change what anyone can do or reach: permissions come from a
person's role and their access grants.

- **Create a team**: give it a **Name** and press **Create team**. You add people afterwards.
- Open a team's row to see who is in it. Choose someone in **Add a member** and press **Add**, or
  press **Remove** beside a person to take them off the team. Neither changes their membership of
  the Organization.
- **Rename**: changes the name; everyone in it stays in it.
- **Delete**: deletes the team and its roster together. Nobody is removed from the Organization.
  Making it again means adding each person back by hand. Press **Delete the team**.

Creating, renaming and deleting Teams, and adding people to them or taking them off, take an owner
or an admin.

## Create a custom role

<Callout type="limit" title="Business plan or Enterprise license">
Custom roles need the Business plan on the Hosted instance, or an Enterprise license on a
self-hosted instance. Creating, editing and deleting them also needs an owner or an admin.
Anyone in the Organization can read the list.
</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Roles">
Open **Settings** > **Roles** and press **Create a role**.

<Screenshot name="roles" alt="The Custom roles card with the role Release manager, its 30 grants across 15 subjects and the one member holding it" caption="Each row says how many grants it has and how many people hold it." />
</Step>
<Step title="Name it">
Type a **Name**, such as `deployer`: lower case, no spaces, at most 50 characters, no commas.
`owner`, `admin`, `member` and the Agent role names are reserved.
</Step>
<Step title="Tick what it allows">
Under **What it allows**, tick each action on each subject. The counter shows how many grants are
ticked. A role with no grants is allowed nothing: people holding it keep their membership and can
read nothing that is checked.

<Screenshot name="roles-builder" alt="The Create a role dialog with the name deployer and Deployment read, create and cancel ticked" caption="Every subject and action comes from the same permission table the backend checks." />
</Step>
<Step title="Create it and assign it">
Press **Create the role**. Then pick it in **Change role** on a member's row, or when you invite
someone. Remember to grant them access with **Manage access**.
</Step>
</Steps>

### The Roles screen

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Role** | The name, and "N grants across M subjects". |
| **Members** | How many people hold it. Press the number to open **Who holds ...**, the list of their addresses. |
| **Created** | When it was made. |
| Actions | **Edit** and **Delete**. |

- **Edit** opens the same dialog as **Create a role**, ending in **Save changes**. Renaming a role
  moves everyone holding it to the new name.
- **Delete** works only while nobody holds the role. "Delete ...?" removes it and its grants; press
  **Delete the role**.
- The footer says how many more you can create. At ten, **Create a role** is off until you delete
  one.

## What does "locked out by a custom role" mean?

Without a valid license or Business plan, Cloudgate cannot resolve a custom role, so a person holding
one is refused **every** permission-checked action, including the ones their role grants. They can
sign in but do nothing.

When that happens:

- A banner on **Members** says "N members are locked out by a custom role", with **See the roles**.
  The same banner on **Roles** has **Go to Members**.
- **Roles** shows "Custom roles allow nothing right now." Each role is marked **Allows nothing**,
  **Edit** is off, and **Change their role** opens next to the people holding it. The roles and
  their grants are kept.
- On the Hosted instance, after **Move to Starter** a strip says "Custom roles stop allowing
  anything on" the move date, with **Keep Business**.

Fix it by moving those people to **Admin** or **Member** in **Change role**, or by upgrading to
Business or activating a license, which makes the roles work again unchanged.

## What does "deactivated" mean?

A person whose identity provider deactivated them over SCIM is marked **deactivated** with "Only
the directory can reactivate them." They cannot sign in, and nothing on this screen reverses it. Their
row keeps its buttons, so you can still **Remove** them. See [SSO and SCIM](/docs/guide/sso-and-scim).

## What you cannot do

<Callout type="limit" title="Limits">
- The owner role cannot be transferred, and there is only ever one owner.
- At most ten custom roles per Organization, and none without the Business plan or an Enterprise
  license.
- A custom role cannot be given membership, invitation, Team or Organization rights.
- Teams group people only; they do not change what anyone can do.
- Invitations cannot be resent; revoke and invite again instead.
- On a self-hosted instance, only the first sign-up arrives without an invitation.
- Agents are managed under Settings > **Agents**, not here.
</Callout>
