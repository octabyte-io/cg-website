Owners and admins can read the audit log under **Settings** > **Audit log**. It lists, newest
first, every create, change, delete and deploy a signed-in member made through the console or the
API, every sign-in and sign-out, and every call an Agent made, whether it succeeded, was refused or
failed.

## What you can do

- Filter by who did it, what kind of thing it was done to, the action, the outcome and a date range.
- Open an entry to see the role the actor held at the time and what changed.
- See everything one Agent did, from a link on its row in Settings > **Agents**.

## Find who changed something

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the audit log">
Open **Settings** > **Audit log**.
<Screenshot name="audit-log" alt="Settings, Audit log for Northwind Labs, listing deploys, a domain change and Agent calls by deploy-bot with their outcomes" caption="Newest first; refused Agent calls appear with the reason." />
</Step>
<Step title="Narrow it down">
Use the filters: **Actor** (**Anyone**, an email, an Agent, or **The platform**), **Kind**,
**Action**, **Outcome** (succeeded, refused or failed), and **From** and **To**. Email and name match
any part of the value; action and kind match exactly.
</Step>
<Step title="Open the entry">
Open a row to see **Who**, **Their role at the time**, **Outcome**, **When**, **Action**,
**Kind**, the name and id of what it was done to, and what changed.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What is recorded

| Recorded | Not recorded |
|---|---|
| Creates, changes, deletes and deploys by members, through the console or the API | A person's reads |
| Sign-ins and sign-outs | Connection tests |
| Every Agent call, allowed, refused (with the reason) or failed | A person's refused requests |
| Actions Cloudgate takes itself, such as applying a template's Plan for you | Calls through the Gateway (see Token spend on the AI model instead) |

## What you cannot do

<Callout type="limit" title="Limits">
- Only owners and admins, or a custom role that allows it, can read the log. Members cannot.
- Entries cannot be edited or removed.
- Entries from a deleted Organization are kept, but they no longer say which Organization they came
  from, and only an owner or admin of the instance organization can read them.
</Callout>
