The console is the web app you sign in to, on the Hosted instance at
`https://cloudgate.octabyte.app` or on your own instance's address. Every screen sits in the same
frame: the **masthead** across the top, the **rail** down the left, the page in the middle, and the
**Dock** along the bottom when you open a shell or a log there. This page walks through each part,
then the Organization home, Help and the Settings index.

## What you can do

- Move between every screen of an Organization from the rail, or jump with quick search.
- Switch Organization, open your account, change the theme and sign out from the masthead.
- Star the Servers, Projects and Environments you use most, and come back to recent ones.
- Keep shells and live logs open in the Dock while you move between pages.
- Find the docs and support from **Help & support**, and every setting from the **Settings** index.

## How is the console laid out?

The console has four fixed parts. The page scrolls; the frame around it stays put.

| Part | Where | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| Masthead | Top | The brand mark (a link to Organization home), the Organization switcher, where you are, and your avatar menu |
| Rail | Left | Quick search, the links for the current level, and the button that collapses the rail |
| Page | Middle | The screen you opened, with any account banner above it |
| Dock | Bottom, when used | Up to four shells and four live logs, kept open across pages |

A link at the very top, **Skip to content**, appears when you press Tab, for keyboard users who want
to go straight to the page.

## The rail

The rail is the console's navigation. At the Organization level it lists, in order:

| Entry | Opens |
|---|---|
| **Organization home** | `/`, the overview described below |
| **Recents** | A fold-out list of the last five Servers, Projects and Environments you opened |
| **Starred** | A fold-out list of what you starred |
| **Servers** | `/servers`, the machines Cloudgate deploys onto. See [Servers](/docs/guide/servers) |
| **Bought Servers** | `/bought-servers`, every Server bought from Cloudgate with its stage, size, backups and monthly price. Hosted only, and only where buying a Server is offered. See [Bought Servers](/docs/guide/bought-servers) |
| **Projects** (under **Build**) | `/projects`; its arrow folds out a list of your Projects |
| **Templates** (under **Build**) | `/templates`, the app catalogue. See [Templates](/docs/guide/templates) |
| **Deployments** (under **Observe**) | `/deployments`, every Deployment in the Organization |
| **Monitoring** (under **Observe**) | `/monitoring`. See [Monitoring](/docs/guide/monitoring) |
| **Requests** (under **Observe**) | `/requests`, the access log read from your Servers. See [Requests](/docs/guide/requests) |
| **Settings** | `/settings`, the index of every setting |
| **Help & support** | `/help`, docs and support |

**Bought Servers** is left out of the rail, and of quick search's `go:` pages, wherever buying a
Server is not offered, which includes every self-hosted instance. Where it is offered, the row is
there even before anything is bought, so the page with **Buy a Server** is easy to find.

A fold-out list that has nothing to show says so: **No Projects yet.**, **Nothing visited yet.** or
**Nothing starred yet.**

### What changes inside a Project or a Server?

When you open a Server, Project, Environment or Service, the rail lists that resource's own tabs
instead of the Organization's links, and starts with a way back: **Back to Servers**, **Back to
Projects**, or back to the parent it sits in. The tabs themselves are covered on each resource's
guide page, for example [Servers](/docs/guide/servers) and
[Projects and Environments](/docs/guide/projects-and-environments).

### Collapse the rail

Press the panel button at the foot of the rail (**Collapse navigation**) to shrink it to icons.
Hovering an icon shows its name, and the Recents and Starred lists open as a flyout beside it.
Press it again (**Expand navigation**) to bring the labels back. The console remembers the choice
in this browser.

On a narrow window or a phone the rail is a drawer. Open it with the panel button at the top left
of the masthead (**Show navigation**); choosing a link, pressing **Esc** or tapping outside closes it.

## The masthead

The masthead runs across the top of every screen.

- **The brand mark** opens Organization home. On a self-hosted instance with its own branding, it
  shows that name and logo instead of Cloudgate's.
- **The Organization switcher**, next to the mark, shows the Organization you are in. Open it to
  switch to another, to make a **New organization**, or to open **Organization settings**. An
  Organization paid for by another one is listed under it, marked **Paid by another
  Organization**. See [Organizations](/docs/guide/organizations).
- **Where you are**: inside a resource, its name with the names of its parents as links (for
  example `storefront / production`), its status badges, and a star. When there are siblings to
  move between, a small arrow (**Switch resource**) lists them. At the Organization level the
  current page's name appears here once you scroll down.
- **Your avatar** opens the account menu.

### The avatar menu

Press your avatar at the top right. The menu has your name at the top and three things:

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Profile** | Opens **Settings** > **Account**, your profile, password, two-factor, sessions and personal keys. See [Sign-in and your account](/docs/guide/sign-in-and-account) |
| **Appearance** | **Light**, **Dark** or **System**. The console opens light on a first visit and remembers your choice in this browser; the sign-in screens follow it too |
| **Sign out** | Ends this session and returns you to sign-in. Other sessions stay signed in |

<Screenshot name="console-avatar-menu" alt="The avatar menu open under the masthead, showing Ada Okafor, Profile, the Appearance choice of Light, Dark and System, and Sign out" caption="Theme and sign-out live in the avatar menu, not in Settings." />

### Star a Server, Project or Environment

Press the star beside a resource's name in the masthead to star it; press it again to unstar it.
Starred items appear under **Starred** in the rail and on Organization home. Stars belong to you
in this browser: they are not shared with your team, and they do not follow you to another
browser. A starred item that is deleted drops off the list.

## Quick search

Quick search finds a Project, an Environment, a Server or a console page by name. Open it with
**Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac), with **Quick search…** at the top of the rail, or with the search box
on Organization home.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open it">
Press **Ctrl+K** or **⌘K** anywhere in the console. With nothing typed, it shows **Search tips**.
</Step>
<Step title="Type">
Type part of a name. Results list the kind (Project, Environment, Server or page), the name and a
hint, such as a Server's address or a Project's description. Up to 40 results show at once.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose">
Move with the arrow keys and press **Enter**, or click a result. **Esc** closes it without going
anywhere.
</Step>
</Steps>

To search one kind only, start with a prefix:

| Prefix | Searches |
|---|---|
| `proj:` | Projects |
| `env:` | Environments, shown as `Project / Environment` |
| `srv:` | Servers |
| `go:` | Console pages, such as Deployments or Settings |

<Callout type="limit" title="What quick search does not find">
It searches names of Projects, Environments, Servers and pages. A Service is not listed on its
own: open its Environment and find it there. If a list could not be loaded, the search says which
one, for example "Servers could not be loaded".
</Callout>

<Screenshot name="console-command-palette" alt="The quick search dialog with env: typed and four Environment results: ai-lab / production, analytics / production, storefront / production and storefront / staging" caption="A prefix narrows the search to one kind." />

## The Dock

The Dock is a panel along the bottom that keeps shells and live logs open while you move between
pages. A shell or a log card's Dock button moves it there; it holds up to four shells and four logs,
each as a tab.

- **Ctrl** with the backtick key (`` ` ``) moves the keyboard between the page and the Dock.
- The panel buttons **Minimise the Dock**, **Maximise the Dock**, **Restore the Dock** and **Close
  the Dock** change its size or close it. Drag its top edge to resize it.
- **+** (**Open another shell**) opens a shell on something the current page offers.
- Closing a shell's tab ends that shell and everything running in it. Closing a log's tab only stops
  showing it; the work it shows carries on. **Close the Dock** with more than one tab asks first.

<Callout type="note" title="When the Dock empties">
Minimising keeps every shell running. Switching Organization or signing out ends every shell and log
in the Dock. When the Dock is full, controls that would add a shell are off with "The Dock holds
four shells. Close one to open another."
</Callout>

## How do tables and file editors fit a narrow screen?

Nothing in the console scrolls sideways except a file you are editing. A table that is wider than
its space, on a phone or in a half-width card, stacks instead: each row becomes a block with its
name and its actions on the first line and the other columns under it as labelled values. Sorting
is a table control, so a stacked list keeps the order it last had. The table comes back once there
is room for it.

An editable file, such as a [Compose file](/docs/guide/compose-stacks), the
[Project config](/docs/guide/project-config) or a [Traefik file](/docs/guide/traefik), does not wrap long lines by default: it scrolls
sideways inside its own box, so YAML indentation stays easy to read and a parser's line and column
match what is on screen. Turn on the **Wrap lines** switch under the editor to wrap them; a
wrapped line keeps its indentation and its one line number. The choice is remembered by this
browser for every editor. Read-only views, such as diffs, converted files and the copies a Plan shows,
always wrap.

## Banners above the page

Some account states show a banner across the top of every page until they are resolved.

| Banner | Who sees it | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Suspended. Contact Octabyte.** | Everyone in the Organization, Hosted only | Nothing new starts until the suspension is lifted; what runs keeps running. Contact Octabyte |
| **This Organization's plan has ended. Pay to continue.** | Everyone, Hosted only | Press **Choose a plan**. See [Billing and plans](/docs/guide/billing) |
| **A payment failed.** | Everyone, Hosted only | Stripe retries it; press **Open billing** and update the card |
| **Your Enterprise license ends on** a date | Owners and admins, self-hosted | An owner renews with Octabyte, then presses **I've renewed, check now** or **Open License**. Before the last days it can be hidden with the cross |
| **Your Enterprise license ended on** a date | Owners and admins, self-hosted | Lists what has stopped until it is renewed |

## Organization home

Organization home, at `/`, is where the console opens after sign-in. Under the heading **Time to
deploy something.** is a search box that opens quick search, then four cards:

| Card | What it shows | When it is empty |
|---|---|---|
| **Servers** | The first five Servers with a status dot, and how many there are in total and how many are reachable. **All** opens `/servers` | **Add a Server**, if your role may add one |
| **Projects** | The first five Projects with how many Environments each has. **All** opens `/projects` | **New Project**, if your role may create one |
| **Recents** | Where you were last, newest first | "Open a Server, a Project or an Environment and it shows up here." |
| **Starred** | What you starred in a masthead | "Star a Server, a Project or an Environment beside its name to keep it here." |

<Screenshot name="home" alt="Organization home for Northwind Labs with the search box under Time to deploy something, and cards for the Servers nyc-edge, build-1 and fra-1, the Projects ai-lab, analytics and storefront, and empty Recents and Starred" caption="The Servers card counts how many Servers are reachable." />

If your role cannot add a Server or create a Project, the button on an empty card is off and says
why when you hover it: ask an owner or an admin. See [Members and roles](/docs/guide/members-and-roles).

## Help & support

**Help & support**, at the foot of the rail, opens `/help`. Every link opens this website in a new
tab.

- **Guides**: [Quickstart](/docs/quickstart), **User guide** (this guide, starting with the
  [get-started wizard](/docs/guide/get-started)), [How Cloudgate fits together](/docs/concepts),
  [CLI guide](/docs/cli), [MCP and agents](/docs/mcp) and the [API reference](/docs/api).
- **Common answers**: [Troubleshooting](/docs/help/troubleshooting), [FAQ](/docs/help/faq),
  [Limits](/docs/help/limits) and the [changelog](/changelog).
- **Contact support**: `support@octabyte.io`, with a copy button and **Email support**. Include your
  Organization's name and what you were doing.
- **All docs** at the top right opens the docs home.

## The Settings index

**Settings** in the rail opens `/settings`: every settings section as a card with one sentence on
what it is for, in groups. On a wide screen each section's page keeps the list beside it; on a phone
a section links back to this index instead.

| Group | Sections |
|---|---|
| **You** | **Account**: your profile, password, two-factor, sessions and personal keys. See [Sign-in and your account](/docs/guide/sign-in-and-account) |
| **Organization** | **Members**, **Agents**, **Organizations**, **Certificates**, **Billing** |
| **Integrations** | **Git providers**, **Registries**, **Cloud accounts**, **Destinations**, **Notifications**, **AI** |
| **Platform** | **Traefik**, **Instance**, **Maintenance**, **Operator** |
| **Enterprise** | **License**, **Audit log**, **Roles**, **Single sign-on**, **Forward auth**, **Branding** |

<Screenshot name="settings-index" alt="The Settings index with the You, Organization, Integrations, Platform and Enterprise groups as cards, each with a one-line description" caption="Each card says what its section is for. This self-hosted owner also sees the Platform group." />

<Callout type="limit" title="Sections you may not see">
**Traefik**, **Instance**, **Maintenance** and **Branding** are about the machine Cloudgate runs
on: a self-hosted instance lists them, and on the Hosted instance only its operators see them.
**Operator** shows only to the Hosted instance's operators. What you may change in any section
depends on your role; see [Members and roles](/docs/guide/members-and-roles) and, for a self-hosted
instance, [Instance settings](/docs/self-host/instance-settings).
</Callout>

## When something goes wrong on a screen

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **That page is not here.** | The address names nothing this Organization has: a mistyped path, a tab that does not exist, or a resource that is gone | Press **Go to Organization home**, or use the rail |
| **This screen could not be shown.** (or the resource's name) | The screen failed while drawing | Press **Try again**. Anything you had already started, such as a deploy, keeps running |
| **You are no longer a member of this Organization** | You were removed, or the Organization was deleted, while signed in | Choose another Organization you belong to from the list, or **Sign out** |
| **You are no longer in any Organization** | You were removed from the only one you had | **Sign out**, then open the link in a new invitation |

A page outside the console, such as a mistyped address while signed out, shows **That page is not
here.** with a link back to the console; signing in takes you to your Organization.
