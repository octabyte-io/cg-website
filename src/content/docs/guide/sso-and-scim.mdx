<Callout type="limit" title="Paid features">
Single sign-on, SCIM and forward auth need the Business plan on the Hosted instance, or an
Enterprise license on a self-hosted instance. Setting them up takes an owner or an admin.
</Callout>

## What you can do

- Sign members in through an OIDC identity provider for your email domains.
- Provision and deactivate members from your directory with SCIM.
- Put an Application's Domain behind a sign-in at your identity provider, with forward auth.
- On a self-hosted instance, turn off password sign-in for everyone.

## Set up single sign-on

Open **Settings** > **Single sign-on**.

<Steps>
<Step title="Prove your email domain (Hosted)">
Under **Proven domains**, type the part of your people's addresses after the @, such as
`northwind.example`. Publish the TXT record it shows at your DNS provider and press **Check now**.
Nothing polls, so press it again once the record is published, until the domain reads **Proven**.
<Screenshot name="sso" alt="Settings, Single sign-on for Northwind Labs, with an OIDC provider, a proven domain northwind.example and trusted origins" caption="Proven domains, identity providers and trusted origins on one screen." />
</Step>
<Step title="Trust the provider's origin">
Under **Trusted origins**, add the scheme and host of your provider, such as
`https://login.northwind.example`, and press **Trust it**. On the Hosted instance it must be a
public address.
</Step>
<Step title="Add the provider">
Under **Identity providers**, press **Add a provider**. In **Add an OIDC provider**, fill in:

- **Provider id**: lower-case letters, numbers and dashes, unique on the whole instance. It cannot
  be changed later.
- **Issuer URL**, **Client id** and **Client secret** from your provider.
- **Email domains**: comma separated, such as `northwind.example`. Members on these domains sign
  in through this provider.
- **Scopes** and endpoints only if your provider needs them; left empty, its own defaults apply.
</Step>
<Step title="Register the redirect URI">
Copy the **Redirect URI** the dialog shows into your identity provider's application settings.
</Step>
</Steps>

People on your proven domains now sign in through your provider. Someone who arrives through SSO
joins only that provider's Organization.

<Callout type="warn" title="Require single sign-on turns off passwords for everyone">
On a self-hosted instance, **Require single sign-on** under **How people sign in** removes the
password form from the sign-in screen for every Organization on the instance, including yours.
Anyone without an account at one of your providers cannot get in. It needs authority over the whole
instance.
</Callout>

## Provision members with SCIM

<Steps>
<Step title="Add a SCIM provider">
Under **Directory provisioning (SCIM)**, press **Add a provider**, give it a **Provider id** and
continue. Have somewhere to paste the token first.
</Step>
<Step title="Copy the base URL and token">
Copy the **SCIM base URL** and the **Bearer token** into your directory. The token is shown once.
</Step>
</Steps>

Your directory now creates members and deactivates leavers. A member deactivated over SCIM cannot
sign in, and only the directory can reactivate them. Removing a SCIM provider stops its token
working immediately.

## Put an Application behind forward auth

Forward auth runs an authentication proxy (oauth2-proxy) on a Server's Traefik, so visitors to a
Domain must sign in at your identity provider first. It needs an OIDC provider under Single
sign-on.

<Steps>
<Step title="Set an authentication domain">
Open **Settings** > **Forward auth**. For the Server, press **Set an authentication domain**,
enter a host such as `auth.northwind.example` and its certificate, then **Save the domain**. Its
parent domain becomes the cookie domain, so it must share one with the Applications it protects,
and it needs DNS pointing at the Server like any other Domain.
</Step>
<Step title="Deploy the proxy">
Press **Deploy the proxy**, choose the provider under **Authenticate against**, and press
**Deploy it**. Only OIDC providers are listed.
</Step>
<Step title="Gate a Domain">
Open the Application's **Domains** tab and turn on the sign-in switch beside the host, such as
`shop.northwind.example`.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="Removing the proxy opens gated Domains">
**Remove the proxy** opens every Domain behind it to anyone with the link.
</Callout>

## What you cannot do

<Callout type="limit" title="Limits">
- SAML providers can be listed but not added or edited in the console.
- On the Hosted instance, a provider signs in only addresses in a proven domain, and its issuer,
  endpoints and trusted origins must be public addresses.
- Compose stacks cannot be put behind forward auth.
- On a lapsed plan or license, the settings are kept: SSO sign-in stops, SCIM shows
  **Removing only**, and forward auth keeps protecting gated Domains but refuses changes.
</Callout>
