Every resource belongs to one Organization, and members act inside it. Each Organization has its
own Projects, Servers, members, integrations and settings; nothing is shared between two of them.

## What you can do

- Create a new Organization and become its owner.
- Switch between the Organizations you belong to, and choose which one you land in at sign-in.
- Rename an Organization, copy its id, or delete it.
- On the Hosted instance, pay for several Organizations from one Business plan.

## Create an Organization

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the switcher">
Open the Organization switcher at the top of the console and press **New organization**. The same
button is on **Settings** > **Organizations**.
<Screenshot name="organizations" alt="Settings, Organizations, listing Northwind Labs as the default with the owner role and a second Organization" caption="You are here marks the current one; Default is where you land at sign-in." />
</Step>
<Step title="Name it">
Give it a name and press **Create Organization**. You will be its owner, and it starts with its own
members: only you. You stay in the Organization you are in.
</Step>
</Steps>

Creating an Organization takes an owner or admin role in the one you are in.

<Callout type="limit" title="How many you can have">
- **Hosted**: a person can own one Organization that pays for itself. Organizations attached to a
  Business plan do not count. See [Billing](/docs/guide/billing).
- **Self-hosted**: one Organization, unless the instance has an Enterprise license. Extra
  Organizations made before still work.
</Callout>

## Switch Organization and set a default

Use **Switch Organization** in the switcher, or **Switch to it** on a row in **Settings** >
**Organizations**. **Make default** sets where you land at sign-in. Switching ends every shell and
log open in the Dock.

## Ownership

Every Organization has one owner, who can do everything, including delete it. The owner role
cannot be transferred. An admin can do everything except delete the Organization. See
[Members and roles](/docs/guide/members-and-roles).

## When an Organization is frozen

<Callout type="limit" title="Hosted only">
On the Hosted instance, an Organization is frozen when its plan has lapsed (a failed payment that
Stripe stopped retrying, a cancelled plan or trial that reached its end) or when Octabyte suspended
it. The console shows a banner: "This Organization's plan has ended. Pay to continue." or
"Suspended. Contact Octabyte."
</Callout>

While frozen:

- Everything already running keeps running on your Servers and in your clouds.
- Nothing new starts: no Deployment, Cron run, backup, Uptime check, threshold or cleanup.
- Members can still sign in, see everything, pay, download configs and delete things.
- Bought Servers are the exception: they are deleted when the Organization lapses.

Paying again lifts a lapse; only Octabyte lifts a suspension.

<Callout type="warn" title="A lapsed Organization is deleted after 30 days">
An Organization that has lapsed, and is not suspended, is deleted 30 days after it lapsed. Pay
before then to keep it.
</Callout>

## Delete an Organization

Open **Settings** > **Organizations** and use **Delete Organization** in the danger zone, typing its
name to confirm.

<Callout type="warn" title="This cannot be undone">
Everything in it goes: Projects, Environments, Services, Servers, members, integrations and
settings. Cloudgate does not touch the machines themselves.
</Callout>

## What you cannot do

<Callout type="limit" title="Limits">
- You cannot delete your only Organization: you have to own at least one.
- A Business Organization that pays for others cannot be deleted while any is attached.
- The owner role cannot be transferred.
</Callout>
