Every resource belongs to one Organization, and members act inside it. Each Organization has its
own Projects, Servers, members, integrations and settings; nothing is shared between two of them.

## What you can do

- Create a new Organization and become its owner.
- Switch between the Organizations you belong to, and choose the one you land in at sign-in.
- Rename an Organization you own, copy its id, or delete it.
- On the Hosted instance, pay for several Organizations from one Business plan.

## The Organizations screen

**Settings** > **Organizations** (`/settings/organizations`) lists every Organization you belong to.

<Screenshot name="organizations" alt="Settings, Organizations for Ada Okafor: Northwind Labs marked You are here and Default, Northwind Sandbox with Switch to it, Make default and Rename, the owner role on both, New organization, and the Danger zone with Delete Organization" caption="You are here marks the current one; Default is where you land at sign-in." />

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Organization** | Its name, **You are here** on the one you are in, **Default** on the one you land in, and its id. |
| **Your role** | Your role in it: `owner`, `admin`, `member` or a custom role. |
| Copy | Copies the Organization's id. |
| Actions | **Switch to it** (not on the current one), **Make default** (not on the default) and **Rename** (only where you are the owner). |

On the Hosted instance a line under the header says how many you own of the one you can own
yourself, for example "You own 1 of 1 you can own yourself. Attached ones you own do not count."

If you belong to none, the card says **You are not in any Organization**: create one, or open the
link in an invitation to join one.

## Create an Organization

<Steps>
<Step title="Open New organization">
Press **New organization** in the Organization switcher at the top of the console, or on
**Settings** > **Organizations**.
</Step>
<Step title="Name it">
In **Create an Organization**, type a **Name** (at most 255 characters) and press **Create
Organization**. You are its owner and only member; it starts empty.
</Step>
<Step title="Switch to it">
You stay in the Organization you are in. Switch to the new one to add its Servers, Projects and
members.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Who can create one?

On the Hosted instance, any signed-in person with a confirmed email address; on a self-hosted
instance, an owner or admin, and a second Organization needs an Enterprise license. When **New
organization** is greyed out, the line under it says why.

| Instance | Who may create | Refused when |
|---|---|---|
| **Hosted** | Any signed-in person with a confirmed email address. | You already own an Organization that pays for itself: "You already own ..., and a person can own one Organization that pays for itself". If that one is on Business, a link offers **Add an Organization to ...'s plan**; otherwise **See plans**. |
| **Self-hosted** | An owner or admin of the Organization you are in. | The instance has no Enterprise license and already has one Organization. Instance administrators see **Go to License**. |

Organizations a Business plan pays for (Attached Organizations) do not count towards the one you
can own yourself. See [Billing](/docs/guide/billing#pay-for-other-organizations-business).

On a self-hosted instance without a license that already has more than one Organization, a notice
says "This instance is over the free version's limit of one Organization. Existing ones keep
working."

## Switch Organization and set a default

- **Switch**: pick an Organization in the switcher at the top of the console, or press **Switch to
  it** on its row. The whole console moves to it, and every open shell into the old one ends.
- **Make default**: sets where you land when you sign in. Without a default, signing in asks which
  Organization to work in.

In the switcher, an Organization paid for by another one you belong to sits indented under it. One
paid for by an Organization you are not in says **Paid by another Organization**. **Organization
settings** at the bottom opens Settings.

## Rename an Organization

Press **Rename** on its row (you must be its owner), type the new **Name** and press **Rename
Organization**. Only the name changes; nothing inside it moves.

## Ownership

Every Organization has one owner, who can do everything, including delete it. The owner role
cannot be transferred. An admin can do everything except delete the Organization and rename it
here. See [Members and roles](/docs/guide/members-and-roles).

## When an Organization is frozen

<Callout type="limit" title="Hosted only">
On the Hosted instance, an Organization is frozen when its plan has lapsed (a failed payment that
Stripe stopped retrying, or a cancelled plan or trial that reached its end) or when Octabyte
suspended it. A self-hosted Organization is never frozen.
</Callout>

A banner at the top of every console page tells every member why:

| Banner | Means |
|---|---|
| **A payment failed.** | Stripe will retry it. Nothing has stopped yet; update the card before the retries run out. **Open billing** goes to Billing. |
| **This Organization's plan has ended. Pay to continue.** | The plan lapsed. **Choose a plan** goes to Billing. |
| **Suspended. Contact Octabyte.** | Octabyte suspended it, with the reason when one was given. Paying does not lift it. |

While frozen:

- Everything already running keeps running on your Servers and in your clouds.
- Nothing new starts: no Deployment, Cron run, backup, Uptime check or Cloud run.
- Members can still sign in, see everything, pay, download configs and delete things.
- Bought Servers are the exception: they are deleted when the Organization's plan lapses.

Paying again lifts a lapse; only Octabyte lifts a suspension.

<Callout type="warn" title="A lapsed Organization is deleted after 30 days">
An Organization that has lapsed, and is not suspended, is deleted 30 days after it lapsed. Pay
before then to keep it.
</Callout>

## Delete an Organization

The danger zone at the foot of **Settings** > **Organizations** is shown only to the owner of the
Organization you are in.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the danger zone">
Under **Delete ...**, press **Delete Organization**. The dialog counts the Projects and Servers it
destroys, with every Environment, Deployment, variable, Domain, certificate, integration and member.
</Step>
<Step title="Type its name">
Type the Organization's name to confirm and press the button. The console moves you to another of
your Organizations and reloads.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="This cannot be undone">
Everything in it goes: Projects, Environments, Services, Servers, members, integrations and
settings. Cloudgate does not go near the machines: containers already running on a Server keep
running, unmanaged, and nothing will be able to reach them again.
</Callout>

## What you cannot do

<Callout type="limit" title="Limits">
- You cannot delete your only Organization: the button says "You have to own at least one
  Organization, and this is your only one."
- A Business Organization that pays for others cannot be deleted while any is attached; stop paying
  for each first.
- The instance's own Organization (the first one on the instance) cannot be deleted.
- Only the owner can rename or delete an Organization.
- The owner role cannot be transferred.
</Callout>
